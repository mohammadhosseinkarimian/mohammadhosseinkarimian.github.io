# Content reference notes

The CV content in this site was prepared from the owner's uploaded PDFs:

- `about(1).pdf` (LinkedIn profile, bio and education)
- `experience(1).pdf` (roles, responsibilities, dates)
- `projects(1).pdf` (project list)
- `skills(1).pdf` (technology and skills)
- `github(1).pdf` (public repositories, profile link and email)

Only CV-relevant material was used. LinkedIn activity feeds and connections were deliberately ignored. The source PDF documents are not included in this website project.

Verified profile destinations:

- GitHub: https://github.com/mohammadhosseinkarimian
- LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/mohammadhossein-karimian/

Verified publication records:

- AAAI 2026: https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v40i23.38992
- Canadian AI 2025: https://doi.org/10.21428/594757db.2243aae3

Other project details were supplemented from the publicly readable LinkedIn project descriptions under the same verified profile. Direct links to unverified or private project repositories were intentionally omitted. The site is editable in one central file: `src/data/portfolio.js`.

The visual design is an original implementation. It uses editorial typography, bento-style project cards, restrained dimensional motion and accessible navigation. No third-party website's source code, imagery or templates were copied.
# Roadframe sources

- Risk model training data: Moura, Daniel C., and Zvitia, Orly. [Nexar Collision Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nexar-ai/nexar_collision_prediction). Hugging Face, 2025. Copyright 2025 Nexar Inc. See the [Nexar Open Data License](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nexar-ai/nexar_collision_prediction/blob/main/LICENSE). The browser exports the trained ResNet-18 encoder and GRU separately so each frame can be encoded once.
- Camera scene classification: [MIT Places365](https://github.com/CSAILVision/places365), ResNet-18. Camera face detection: [MediaPipe BlazeFace](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/face_detector). Browser inference uses [ONNX Runtime Web](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime).
- Traffic buildup: [Driving from Broad Creek to Jennifer Road in Annapolis](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Driving_from_Broad_Creek_to_Jennifer_Road_in_Annapolis,_Maryland_(1_June_2026).webm) by Illegitimate Barrister. Quiet road: [ROAD VIEW](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ROAD_VIEW.webm) by Niyati Sharma 1505. Both video excerpts and poster stills are trimmed or resized and distributed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- Highway collision: [Wikimedia Commons dashcam footage](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_speeding_car_rear-ended_into_the_truck_in_front,_and_the_front_hood_was_blown_open.webm), trimmed and resized. The approximate impact marker is manually annotated. All sample scores were generated by the browser model.
# Find Products sources

- OCR training uses the [TextOCR annotations](https://textvqa.org/textocr/dataset/) and [EasyOCR 1.7.2 Latin model](https://github.com/JaidedAI/EasyOCR/tree/v1.7.2). Browser detection and the second recognizer use the [PaddleOCR PP-OCRv5 mobile ONNX exports](https://huggingface.co/nakamura196/ppocrv5-mobile-web-onnx) (Apache 2.0). Barcode decoding uses [ZXing for JavaScript](https://github.com/zxing-js/library).
- The searchable product index is a subset of [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/data) via the [Canada snapshot](https://huggingface.co/datasets/offCanada/openfoodfacts-canada). The compact index retains product names, French alternatives, barcodes, brands, quantities, photo URLs and Nutri-Score. It is available under the [Open Database License](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). Barcode and brand lookups use the Open Food Facts API. Dated Canadian price reports come from [Open Prices](https://prices.openfoodfacts.org/).
- The sample montage incorporates photos by Open Food Facts contributors for [No Name hamburger buns](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/product/0060383022921), [Nutella](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/product/3017620422003), and [Ozery sandwich buns](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/product/0664164100411). The montage is distributed under [CC BY-SA](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). The date stamp is illustrative, and the sample outlines were generated by the browser OCR pipeline.
